Version 0.9.0-beta.4

1. THIS IS A BETA

LiberCast is unfinished software being tested. It may crash, lose settings, or
stop working after an update. Do not rely on it for anything that matters, and
do not use it as your only way to record something you cannot miss.


2. LIBERCAST DOES NOT PROVIDE CHANNELS OR CONTENT

LiberCast is a player. It contains no channels, no playlists, no accounts and
no subscriptions, and it is not affiliated with any provider of those. It plays
what you point it at.

You are responsible for what you play through it. By installing, you confirm
that you have the right to access the streams, playlists and accounts you use
with it, and that you will not use LiberCast to access content you are not
entitled to. Nothing here grants you any right to any content.

4. WHAT IT DOES WITH YOUR DATA

Nothing leaves your computer except the requests you ask for. LiberCast has no
analytics, no telemetry and no account of its own. It talks to the provider you
configure, to the guide URL you configure, and to nothing else.

The one exception is one you are asked about: on first launch LiberCast offers
to check GitHub once a day for a newer version. It asks for a version number
and sends nothing about you. Say no and it never makes that request.

Your provider passwords are encrypted with Windows DPAPI, tied to the Windows
account you are signed in as, and kept in your own user profile:

    %APPDATA%\LiberCast

Uninstalling leaves that folder in place so a reinstall keeps your sources. To
remove everything, delete that folder by hand after uninstalling.
